Know Your Farm: Myth vs Fact認識你嘅農場:迷思 vs 事實

"Organic means no pesticides at all."「有機就係完全冇農藥。」

Fact: Organic farms avoid synthetic pesticides and chemical fertilisers. Some natural substances may be allowed under certification rules, so ask your farmer what they use.事實:有機農場避免使用合成農藥同化學肥料。認證規則下可能容許部分天然物質,所以可以問農夫用咗咩。

"Organic vegetables look perfect."「有機蔬菜應該好靚。」

Fact: Shapes and sizes vary, and small insect marks can happen. It is a sign of real farming.事實:形狀同大小各有不同,亦可能有細小蟲咬痕跡,係真實耕種嘅表現。

Meet the Farmer認識農夫

Featured farmer農夫專訪

👩‍🌾 Flora · "Follow nature, keep it pure"· 「順應自然,堅守純淨」

Farm:農場: Blue Girl Organic Farm藍妹有機農莊 Blue Girl Organic Farm

🌱 100% pure100% 純淨

Blue Girl Organic Farm, Flora's farm, has been certified by the Hong Kong Organic Resource Centre for 13 years in a row. She uses no chemical pesticides or fertilisers at all. She believes only crops grown by following nature carry the gentleness of the land and their natural freshness.Flora 嘅農莊 Blue Girl Organic Farm 已經連續 13 年獲香港有機資源中心認證。佢堅持完全唔用任何化學農藥同肥料,認為只有「順應自然」種出嚟嘅作物,先有大地嘅溫柔同天然嘅鮮味。

🥬 Proud of the taste對味道好有信心

She is very confident in her vegetables. In summer, she says, conventionally grown Malabar spinach (潺菜) often has a heavy earthy "grey" taste that children dislike. Her organic version has no off-taste, and is smoother and tastier.Flora 對自己種嘅菜好有信心。佢以夏天嘅潺菜為例,話常規種植嘅菜往往有一種好重、小朋友唔鍾意嘅「灰味」(泥腥味);而純有機種出嚟嘅潺菜冇異味,口感更滑嫩、味道更好。

💪 Hard work every day親力親為嘅辛勞

Running a production farm of over 100,000 square feet is not easy. From planting seedlings, watering and weeding to fertilising and harvesting, there is hardly a moment to rest. She once went two or three years without a day off, and in summer she gets up at 4 a.m. to avoid the heat.經營一個超過十萬呎嘅生產型農場極不容易。由落苗、淋水、除草、施肥到收割,幾乎冇一刻可以偷懶。Flora 試過兩三年冇放過假,夏天為咗避開酷熱,凌晨四點就要起身工作。

What We Want to Say: Our Social Mission想表達的內容與社會使命

🌾 Do not force nature唔好強迫大自然

Growing out-of-season vegetables with heavy fertiliser and greenhouses treats the land as a tool. "When we are picky eaters, the soil is also picky about its fertiliser." Eating in season is kinder to the land.用大量肥料同温室種反季節蔬菜,係將土地當成工具。「當我哋偏食,土地都喺度偏食肥料。」跟住季節食,對土地更好。

🌀 Accept gains and losses坦然面對得失

Typhoons can flatten a whole harvest. Seasonal food reminds us of the power of nature, and teaches us contentment and gratitude.颱風可以令成季收成冇晒。時令食物提醒我哋大自然嘅力量,學會知足同感恩。

🧒 Value every grain珍惜每一粒米

When children and adults work in the field, they understand that every grain takes effort, and eat with joy and thanks.大人細路落田親身體驗,就明白「粒粒皆辛苦」,帶住喜樂同感恩嘅心食飯。
